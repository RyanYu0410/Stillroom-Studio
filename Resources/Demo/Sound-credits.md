The following field recordings are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as verified on the original Freesound pages on 8 November 2026. Their publicly available high-quality MP3 previews are bundled in `assets/recordings/`. These are source recordings for the edit, not claims that sound was captured on the fictional film set.

Narration and dialogue: local Kokoro synthetic voices, one unblended voice for Mara (bf_emma) and a distinct voice for her mother (bf_lily). Each paragraph or dialogue turn is synthesized as a continuous performance with native phoneme timing. The new speech is generated at its final delivery speed and receives no subsequent tempo change. No human voice was cloned for this film.

Music: six sparse original synthesized struck-string notes, created for this film. The previous continuous bass drone has been removed. The uncertainty exchange plays without music. Early room reflections are applied lightly to spoken dialogue; inner narration stays close and dry.

Mastering: two-pass loudness target −18 LUFS, true-peak ceiling −2 dBTP. The final encoded-file measurement is recorded in `source/Validation.json`.
